TankHank is a gas tank bib that slips over your fuel filler neck and catches that last drop of fuel before it runs down your car.

Get a TankHankHow it works

That last drop does damage

Pull the nozzle and there's always a drip. It runs down the fender, dries, and over time stains and dulls the paint around your fuel door. On a car you care about, that adds up.

How it works

1
Slip it onThe circular cutout fits over the fuel filler neck so the bib hangs against the paint below.
2
Fill upPump like normal. Drips land on the bib instead of your finish.
3
Fold it awayIt stays on the filler neck and folds into the fuel door — nothing to dig out of the glove box.
